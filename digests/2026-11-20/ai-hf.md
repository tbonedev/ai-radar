## 3. Ecosystem Signal

**Qwen is the center of gravity.** At least seven entries are built on Qwen3.8-27B or the qwen3_5 architecture. They include the base model, Unsloth's GGUF, two uncensored GGUFs, a GRPO adapter, DaisyRose-27B and Cloudflare's clairic. Qwen-Image and Qwen3-ASR derivatives show the family's reach beyond text.

**Quantized builds draw more downloads than their bases.** Unsloth's GGUF (7.38M downloads) and the abliterated GGUF (4.45M) show strong demand for local inference. Uncensored and abliterated variants get heavy traffic, which suggests many users are looking for fewer refusals.

**Open weights are broad.** Open weights cover video (LTX-2.5, FastFrames), image (FLUX.3 Klein, MiniMax-H3), speech (Haru-4B, ZONOS3) and segmentation (SAM 3). Chinese labs (Qwen, DeepSeek, Tencent, MiniMax, Meituan, SenseNova) account for a large part of the language and multimodal releases. Small, efficient models from NVIDIA, Microsoft and HuggingFace also appear.
